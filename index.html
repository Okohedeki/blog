Edeki Okoh

Software engineer, recovering wet-lab researcher, CRPG addict.

I build software around databases, AI systems, agent infrastructure, and financial services.

I earned a dual degree in mathematics and economics from Arizona State University. As an undergraduate, I researched HIV vaccine vectors in Professor Bertram Jacobs’s lab at the Biodesign Institute—and learned that I hated working in wet labs.

I moved to Professor Carl Gardner’s computational mathematics group, where I modeled the irradiated astrophysical jets HH 901 and HH 902. That work made the direction clear: less pipetting, more computation.

I started my career as an accounting consultant specializing in stock-based compensation. Today, I work on the financial-services software team at a payroll company.

Outside work, I build strange software and lose unreasonable amounts of time to CRPGs.
