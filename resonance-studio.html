Resonance Studio

Speech, acoustics, and video in one local studio.

The idea

A recording contains several people, but sometimes you want to follow just one voice. Resonance brings speaker listening into the same workspace as speech editing and acoustics.

The current workflow

Open a video or audio recording, analyze its speakers, assign names, and switch between the original mix and a selected voice. Playback stays synchronized with the video, and individual tracks can be exported as WAV files.

What I’m exploring

Combining local audio processing with a desktop interface that makes analysis, listening, and comparison understandable. The application uses React and Tauri, with a separate Python inference worker.

Where it stands

This is a prototype for clips up to five minutes, including separation of detected two-voice overlap. Speaker names are assigned by the user. Crowded or difficult speech can be misattributed, and separated tracks can contain leakage or artifacts.

The Windows build is unsigned; models and the inference worker are separate. This page is a project preview, not a public download.
